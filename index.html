Data Sources, Governance & Hackathon Limitations

1. Primary Data Sources

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM) & Overpass API: Community-contributed healthcare facility geometries, nodes, and metadata for the Nagpur administrative region.
  • Nominatim OpenStreetMap: Public geocoding engine for resolving locality names to coordinates.
  • Curated Nagpur Verified Registry: Primary public healthcare landmarks (AIIMS Nagpur, GMCH, IGGMCH Mayo Hospital, Kingsway, Orange City Hospital, Wockhardt, Care Hospital, etc.).

2. Data Quality & Limitations Notice

  • Completeness Disclaimer: This platform does NOT claim that every nursing home or clinic in Nagpur is indexed. Open-source data relies on volunteer mapping and public registry availability.
  • No Live Bed or Doctor Capacity: CareMap does not claim real-time bed availability, ICU status, or on-duty doctor attendance. These require hospital information system (HIS) integration.
  • Emergency Status: The emergency tag indicates recorded 24/7 or emergency infrastructure in OSM, not real-time admission eligibility. Always dial 108 in critical emergencies.
  • Sample Simulation Records: Fictional records are clearly labeled as Sample Simulation to prevent deceptive metrics.
